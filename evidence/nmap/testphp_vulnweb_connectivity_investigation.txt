=== Cross-check from an independent third network (Claude's own browser tool) ===
Both http:// and https://testphp.vulnweb.com failed to load.
Control site (example.com) loaded instantly on the same tool, confirming the
tool's network path itself was fine.

Conclusion: the target site itself was down/unreachable at the time of
testing — not a Kali VM, ISP, or Nmap-fingerprinting issue. Three independent
vantage points (Kali Nmap, Kali curl, unrelated third-party browser) all
failed on this one target while succeeding on everything else. Assessment
scope was switched to demo.testfire.net as a result (see README.md).
